Tips for a clean transcription

  • Sing on a syllable like “da” or “la”. The consonant helps it split repeated notes.
  • One voice, one note at a time. Chords and background music confuse it.
  • Set the tempo first, then sing along with the click. Headphones keep the click out of the mic.
  • If short notes go missing, choose a shorter “Shortest note”. If you get extra tiny notes, choose a longer one.
  • Background noise showing up as notes? Move “Ignore quiet sounds” to the right.
